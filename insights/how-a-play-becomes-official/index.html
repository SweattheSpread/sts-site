How a play becomes official.

What this covers: the steps every Sweat the Spread pick goes through before it is posted, and how it is graded after. What it doesn't cover: any current model numbers.

Step by step

From price to pick

  1. 01

    Our number first

    The model prices the game before anyone looks at the book, so the market can't anchor it.

  2. 02

    The 4% bar

    Our price is compared to a sharp no-vig price. The play is official only if the edge clears 4%.

  3. 03

    Sized in units

    1u standard and max. Gap plays up to 2u. Prices longer than +200 at 0.5u.

  4. 04

    Posted, then graded

    It goes out free on this site and in Telegram before the game, then gets graded at the posted price.

Why it works this way

A system, not a feeling

The rule is the same in every sport. If the edge doesn't clear the bar, there is no play, and passing is a position. Nothing is published as a "lean" and then quietly dropped.

Every published play is official and goes on the record, wins and losses. Results are tracked in units, never dollars, so the record means the same thing no matter what anyone bets.
