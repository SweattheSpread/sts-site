How the Sweat the Spread college basketball model approaches a slate, which plays become official, and where each one is graded.

What this page is

What it prices

  • The model builds its own projection for each game, including the final score, the spread and the total, before we look at the market.
  • That number is compared to a sharp no-vig price. A college basketball play is official only when the edge clears 4%, the same bar as every other sport.
  • Official college basketball plays on the record have mostly been totals and spreads, with the occasional moneyline.
  • Plays are sized in units: 1u standard, gap plays up to 2u, prices longer than +200 at 0.5u.
  • The season runs from November into the spring tournaments, so most college basketball plays land in that window.

This page explains the approach. It does not publish current model numbers or the day's board; those are in the paid Daily Betting Breakdown.
