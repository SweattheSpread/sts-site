Methodology

How a price gets made

  1. 01

    Build our own number

    The model prices the game before we look at the book.

  2. 02

    Compare to sharp no‑vig

    A play is official only when the edge clears 4%.

  3. 03

    Size in units

    1u standard and max. Gap plays up to 2u. Prices longer than +200 at 0.5u.

  4. 04

    Grade everything

    Every published play is official and goes on the record.

If nothing clears the bar, there is no play. Passing is a position.
