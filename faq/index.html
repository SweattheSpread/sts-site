FAQ

  • Are the official picks free?Yes. Every official pick is posted free on this website and in the Sweat the Spread Telegram channel. The paid product is the Daily Betting Breakdown, a model PDF per sport each morning.
  • What makes a play official?The model prices the game first. We then compare that number to a sharp no-vig price. A play is official only when the edge clears 4%. Every published play is official and goes on the record.
  • What is a unit?A unit (1u) is the standard stake size we track results in. 1u is standard and max. Gap plays go up to 2u, and prices longer than +200 are sized at 0.5u. Results are reported in units, never dollars.
  • How is a play graded?After the game is final, each official play is graded at the price we posted. A win adds the units it paid, a loss subtracts the units risked, and voids are kept out of the totals. Pending plays are listed on Results but not counted until they are graded.
  • How is ROI calculated?ROI is units won divided by units risked on graded official plays.
  • Which sports do you cover?Most official plays come from the NFL, college basketball, the NHL, college football, the NBA, tennis and soccer, with other leagues when a play clears the bar. The full list is in the sport filter on Results.
  • Where can I see every past play?On the Results page. It lists every official play since launch, in units, with filters for sport, date, market, result and player props.
  • Is this a guarantee?No. There are no locks and no guarantees. Variance is real. Past results are a report, not a forecast. Bet only what you can afford to lose.
  • Who can use this site?This site is for adults 21 and older. If gambling stops being fun, stop. If you or someone you know has a gambling problem, call 1-800-GAMBLER.
  • How do I get the Daily Betting Breakdown or ask a question?See Pricing and the Breakdown page, or email sweatthespread@stsanalytics.co.
