Responsible gambling

21+. Bet within limits.

Sports betting is entertainment with real risk. Here is how we think about it, and where to get help.

The ground rules

Before you bet

  • You must be 21 or older, and sports betting must be legal where you are. Laws differ by state.
  • Past results are a report, not a forecast. No pick is a lock and nothing is guaranteed.
  • Bet only what you can afford to lose. Set a budget before you start and keep to it.
  • Don't chase losses. Losing streaks happen to every model. Variance is real.
  • Track every bet in units, the same way we track ours. If you can't see your own record, you can't manage it.
  • Take breaks. If betting stops being fun, stop.

Help is free and confidential

Get help

  • Call 1-800-GAMBLERThe national problem gambling helpline, available any time.
  • National Council on Problem GamblingHelp, treatment options and resources by state.
  • Gamblers AnonymousFree peer support meetings, in person and online.
  • Self-exclusionLicensed sportsbooks offer deposit limits, time-outs and self-exclusion in your account settings. Use them.
