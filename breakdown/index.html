What's inside each sport's PDF

Every game, two pages

  • Cover & today's cardGames analyzed, card plays, passes, and any play that cleared +4% vs the sharp no-vig price. If nothing clears, it says so: passing is a position.
  • Model projectionThe model's projected final score, total and spread for every game, plus the model lean.
  • Odds boardSpread, total and moneyline from BetMGM, Caesars and Pinnacle, with Pinnacle fair (no-vig) prices.
  • Model-vs-line gapsWhere the model's spread or total sits against the market number.
  • Matchup, rest & spotMatchup stats, defense allowed, rest and schedule spot, head-to-head. NHL adds projected starters and goalies.
  • Injury reportPulled from ESPN for each game.
  • The readHow we see it playing out, and every lean or play for the game.
